Why it's different

A builder that shows its work

🧠

Reads your system first

POWER looks at what you've connected and the shape of your real events before it suggests anything. No generic templates.

📄

Output is a plain file

What it builds is readable text in your repo. Prefer typing? Edit it by hand. Prefer chat? Ask for the change.

🧪

Dry-run before anything's live

Every build is tested against sample events first. You see each step's output before it touches a real system.

🔐

An approval gate that can't be skipped

Anything generated by AI needs a person's yes before it can act. That's an engine rule, not a setting.

🔁

Every change is a diff

See exactly what changed, line by line, like a code review. Undo is one click, because it's just git.

🏠

Runs on a model you host

Point POWER at a local model. Your alerts, your logs and your prompts never have to leave your network.
